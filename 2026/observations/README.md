# Observations

Dated field notes from checking on the garden — problems noticed, decisions made, photos worth keeping. Each observation is one file:

```
YYYY-MM-DD-<subject>.md
```

Keep observations narrow and factual: what I saw, what I did (or plan to), and when the next check-in is. Cross-link to [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md), [`CARE.md`](../CARE.md), [`PESTS.md`](../PESTS.md), [`WEATHER.md`](../WEATHER.md), or [`SCHEDULE.md`](../SCHEDULE.md) when the observation triggers a change or answers an open question. Photos live in [`../photos/`](../photos/) and are referenced by relative link.

## What goes here vs. elsewhere

- **Here (`observations/`):** dated field notes about a specific plant or bed on a specific day, with a photo when useful.
- **`PESTS.md`:** pest-specific observations tied to the perennial pest log — a dated aphid sighting belongs there, not here.
- **`WEATHER.md`:** significant weather events (freezes, storms, hail) — here only if the observation is a plant's *response* to weather.
- **`SCHEDULE.md`:** near-term tasks that came out of an observation. Link back to the observation file so the reason survives.
- **`PLAN.md` decisions log:** any decision the observation forces (e.g. changing an irrigation zone assignment). Link back to the observation.
