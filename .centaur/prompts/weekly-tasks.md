Post this week's garden tasks to the household group.

1. Take today's date and read `SCHEDULE.md`,
   `CARE.md` and the frost dates in `PLAN.md` in the current season folder (`<YYYY>/`, per CONVENTIONS.md).
2. Reply with a short list of this week's tasks from the matching "Week of" or month section, then
   a line for anything time-sensitive in the next two weeks: frost or cover needs, planting windows,
   watering or irrigation changes, fertilizer, pest checks. Use bed names and crops as the repo
   does, and point to `CARE.md` sections instead of repeating them. Keep it to roughly ten lines of
   plain text.
3. Then send a second, separate message to the household group titled "Needs your attention", with
   at most about eight short bullets, grouped under these labels and omitting any empty group:
   - **Check-ins:** specific plants, beds or pests you need an update on (e.g. germination,
     vigor, pest sightings, harvest readiness), drawn from `CROPS.md`, `PESTS.md` and `WEATHER.md`.
     Ask a concrete question for each so a one-line reply is enough.
   - **Decisions:** open choices from `PLAN.md`, `CROPS.md` or `SCHEDULE.md` that must be settled
     soon (purchases, variety or bed choices, timing calls), each with the deadline and your
     recommended option.
   - **Actions this week:** the hands-on tasks from step 2 that only a person can do, one line
     each, with bed names.
   Do not repeat anything already answered in the repo, and point to files instead of quoting them.
4. If `SCHEDULE.md` is stale (its **Generated:** date is more than a month old or its window no
   longer covers today) or this week is missing, say so in the message, give your best tasks from
   `CARE.md` and `PLAN.md`, and propose a refreshed schedule: regenerate `SCHEDULE.md` per
   `CONVENTIONS.md` and the `garden-calendar-schedule` skill so it goes up as a pull request.
   Do not claim the schedule was updated unless you edited it.
